# Product

Status: product/design target for a pre-alpha repository. Fixture UI and
technical foundations exist, but no approved production ruleset or authoritative
deterministic analyzer is active.

## Register

product

## Users

Path of Exile players who deliberately submit their own build material and want
to understand what to improve without surrendering judgment to a black box.
They compare dense evidence, often on desktop, then use concise recipes on a
second screen or mobile device while manually operating the official Trade UI.

## Product Purpose

Lootwright is designed to turn user-supplied goals and PoB, PoB2, or item text
into edition-scoped deterministic findings, prioritized upgrades, and manual
Trade filter recipes. Success means the player can see what is known, why a
change is recommended, which exact ruleset produced it, what remains uncertain,
and what Lootwright explicitly refuses to automate. The complete core remains
useful with AI and external integrations disabled.

## Brand Personality

Precise, candid, crafted. Lootwright should feel like a trusted technical
workbench: rigorous enough for expert players, calm enough for a first-time
analysis, and direct about uncertainty and policy limits.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI tool marketing, chat-first layouts, glowing purple gradients, and
  claims that imply model authority.
- Official Path of Exile or Trade UI imitation, copied fantasy ornament,
  publisher logos, item art, passive-tree imagery, or screenshot-led design.
- Price-check overlays, urgency counters, ads, sponsored ranking, paywalls,
  donation pressure, and game-client companion aesthetics.
- Dense dashboards that hide evidence behind unexplained scores or present
  estimates as facts.

## Design Principles

- Show the evidence chain, not just the answer.
- Make game edition impossible to overlook at every decision boundary.
- Separate deterministic calculation, AI-authored wording, and policy status.
- Preserve player agency with manual, reversible actions and plain language.
- Give uncertainty, limitations, empty states, and refusal states first-class
  space instead of treating them as failures to hide.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Every flow must work by keyboard, expose visible focus,
retain meaning without color, support reduced motion, use semantic headings and
landmarks, maintain readable contrast and line length, and remain functional
from 320px mobile layouts through wide desktop workspaces. Turkish is the
default locale foundation and English is available without changing result
semantics.

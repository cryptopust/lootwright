# Project Impact

Status: intended impact and measurement plan, not a claim of achieved scale or
OpenAI/GGG support.

## Problem

Build-planning tools can be difficult to interpret, especially when estimates,
game editions, patch assumptions, and market claims are mixed together.
Turkish-speaking players also have fewer high-quality technical explanations.
Lootwright aims to make the reasoning visible: what was parsed, which exact
ruleset was used, what finding triggered a recommendation, and what remains
uncertain.

## Public-benefit intent

- Provide the same deterministic product in Turkish and English.
- Teach users why a recommendation exists rather than presenting an opaque
  score or fabricated certainty.
- Preserve useful AI-off operation for users who decline provider processing or
  when cost/policy disables it.
- Publish original code, schemas, test fixtures, security controls, and eval
  methods under the project's stated license scope.
- Avoid live-market surveillance, trade automation, paywalls, advertising,
  sponsored rankings, and donation-linked advantage.

## Impact measures

The project would report only aggregate, privacy-minimized measures such as:

- deterministic analysis completion and fallback rates;
- clarification resolution rates by locale;
- accessibility and comprehension test outcomes;
- unknown-reference and policy-denial counts;
- AI opt-in rate, cache rate, token percentiles, and cost totals; and
- reproducible eval pass/fail counts by version.

Player/build content, account identities, supporter identity, and private notes
are excluded from impact reporting. Traffic scenarios in the cost model are
projections, not user claims.

## Independence

No contribution, credit, or sponsorship would influence recommendations,
rankings, quotas, accuracy, support response time, or community visibility.
Any future acknowledgement would be factual, non-promotional, and would state
that support does not constitute product endorsement unless the provider
explicitly requires and authorizes different wording.

> This product isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Grinding Gear Games in any way.
